BISMILLAH MOBILE & LAPTOP SHOP — DEMONSTRATION SCRIPT
Target length: 5-7 minutes

1. INTRODUCTION (15 sec)
   "This is Bismillah Mobile & Laptop Shop — a full-stack PHP/MySQL e-commerce
   platform for a local electronics business, with an AI shopping assistant,
   real authentication, and a fully custom design system."

2. HOMEPAGE (40 sec)
   - Show hero, trust strip, auto-sliding shop photo
   - Scroll through Featured Products (point out category badges, star ratings,
     image slider with zoom)
   - Show "On Sale Now" section if any product has a discount
   - Show Popular Services and stats band

3. PRODUCTS PAGE (45 sec)
   - Search for a product by name
   - Use the sort dropdown (Price Low to High)
   - Set a min/max price range
   - Click a category filter, show it combines with the other filters
   - Click into a product

4. PRODUCT DETAILS (45 sec)
   - Show breadcrumb trail
   - Click the main photo to open the zoom lightbox, click again to zoom in
   - Scroll to reviews, show existing reviews, submit a new one
   - Scroll to "You Might Also Like"
   - Click "Chat on WhatsApp" to show the pre-filled message

5. AI SHOPPING ASSISTANT (30 sec)
   - Click the chat bubble
   - Ask "What laptops do you have under Rs. 150,000?"
   - Ask something off-topic to show it stays on-topic

6. CONTACT FORM (20 sec)
   - Submit a message, show validation on an empty field
   - Show the success message

7. ADMIN LOGIN & SECURITY (30 sec)
   - Show an invalid login being rejected
   - Log in successfully
   - Briefly mention: Forgot Password sends a real email, and 5 failed
     attempts locks the account for 15 minutes

8. ADMIN DASHBOARD (30 sec)
   - Show the 5 color-coded stat cards
   - Show the Low Stock Warning (if any) and the two charts
   - Show Recent Products / Recent Messages widgets

9. PRODUCT MANAGEMENT (45 sec)
   - Add a product with a main photo + 2 extra photos
   - Show it appears with slider on the public site
   - Edit it, set a sale price, show the discount badge appears
   - Show the QR code (if built) or skip if not
   - Delete it, show the confirmation

10. REVIEWS & MESSAGES (20 sec)
    - Approve a pending review
    - Open a message, show it switches from Unread to Read

11. MY PROFILE (15 sec)
    - Show the password change form with show/hide toggle

12. DATABASE (20 sec)
    - Open phpMyAdmin, show the full table list
    - Point out the products -> categories foreign key relationship

13. WRAP-UP (10 sec)
    "That covers the public storefront, the AI assistant, full CRUD with
    multi-photo uploads, real email-based authentication, and a complete
    admin analytics dashboard — all built, tested, and secured end-to-end."
